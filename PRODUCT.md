# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Suhail Malik for product-engineering roles, along with prospective collaborators. They need a quick, credible understanding of the scope of his work, technical depth, product judgment, and the measurable outcomes he can own.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio turns a work history into concise evidence of product-minded engineering: an individual who can take ambiguous, high-leverage problems from discovery through implementation and measurable outcomes. Success means prompting a relevant hiring conversation or interview.

## Brand Personality

Confident, technical, product-minded, and AI-native. The experience should feel precise and considered rather than corporate, flashy, or self-promotional. It should inspire confidence in both craft and ownership.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template portfolios, dashboard-like SaaS aesthetics, inflated claims without evidence, and AI-generated visual clichés. Do not make technical work feel dry, overly academic, or inaccessible to non-engineering recruiters.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with evidence: connect every claimed strength to a concrete project, decision, or outcome.
2. Make high-signal scanning effortless: recruiters should grasp the value proposition and strongest proof in seconds.
3. Translate technical depth into product consequence, without flattening the engineering.
4. Show independent judgment: AI-native work should read as thoughtful leverage, not novelty.
5. Invite the next conversation with a clear, low-friction path to contact or resume details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve readable contrast, keyboard access, semantic landmarks and headings, descriptive image alternatives, and a reduced-motion experience. The portfolio should work well for readers using zoom, small screens, and assistive technology.
